# Changelog

## 0.4.9 - 2026-09-01

- The Gen 2 Pocket skin keeps the responsive widescreen surface while its
  fixed 160x144 Pack panel is centred within it, removing the left alignment
  without shrinking the UI.
- Responsive Bag overlays now translate their full-colour protection regions
  with their visible content. Typed Move Colors therefore keeps complete,
  stable move-card colours on the wide TM move-learning screen.
- Active pockets now validate their visible item membership as well as the
  inventory quantity signature. If an item-use or companion controller
  replaces a filtered pocket with the native all-items list, the selected
  pocket is restored before the Bag is drawn again.
